 INDICATION
 Patient with imaging-confirmed right paracentral L4-L5 disc protrusion and clinically corroborated right L5 radiculopathy,
 refractory to eight weeks of oral medication and fourteen sessions of physical therapy. Risks, benefits, and alternatives including
 continued conservative care and surgical decompression were discussed. Written informed consent obtained.

 TECHNIQUE
 Patient placed prone on the fluoroscopy table with a bolster beneath the abdomen. The lumbosacral region was prepped with
 chlorhexidine and draped in sterile fashion. Under intermittent fluoroscopic guidance the target foramen was identified and the
 C-arm obliqued to visualize the 'Scotty dog' view. Skin and subcutaneous tissue infiltrated with 1% lidocaine.
 A 22-gauge spinal needle was advanced under intermittent fluoroscopy to the safe triangle beneath the pedicle. Position confirmed
 on AP and lateral views. Negative aspiration for blood and cerebrospinal fluid. Omnipaque 180 contrast, 1 mL, injected
 demonstrating appropriate epidural and perineural spread along the nerve root without vascular uptake.
 A solution of dexamethasone 10 mg with 1 mL of 0.25% bupivacaine was then injected slowly. The patient tolerated the injection
 well and reported his typical radicular pain reproduced on contrast injection, then relieved.

 POST-PROCEDURE
 Patient monitored in recovery for 30 minutes. Motor and sensory function intact bilaterally. Ambulated without difficulty.
 Discharged home in stable condition with a responsible adult driver.
 Pre-procedure pain 7/10. Immediate post-procedure pain 2/10 (anesthetic phase).

 PLAN
  –   Pain diary for 14 days.
  –   Resume physical therapy in 3 days.
  –   Follow up in 3 weeks. May consider a second injection if partial but incomplete relief.
  –   Return to Dr. Halloway if relief is inadequate to discuss surgical options.
